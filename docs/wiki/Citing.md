# Citing

## The atlases

Both built-in atlases are included with their authors' permission. **If you use either one in research, cite it.**

### Salmon atlas

The salmon brain atlas was written by **Marco A. Vindas** and is unpublished. Any research that uses it, including results produced with this app on the salmon atlas, must cite Marco A. Vindas as its author. Until it is published, cite it as:

> Vindas, M. A. Atlantic salmon brain atlas. Unpublished.

### Wullimann zebrafish atlas

> Wullimann, M. F., Rupp, B., & Reichert, H. (1996). *Neuroanatomy of the Zebrafish Brain: A Topological Atlas*. Basel: Birkhäuser Verlag. ISBN 3-7643-5120-9. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-0348-8979-7

### Your own atlas

If it comes from a publication, cite that publication.

## The tools inside the app

Nuclei are found with **StarDist**, used as published. Cite it if you report counts:

> Schmidt, U., Weigert, M., Broaddus, C., & Myers, G. (2018). Cell detection with star-convex polygons. *MICCAI 2018*. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-00934-2_30

The repository's `REFERENCES.md` lists every method the pipeline relies on, and `docs/software_versions.md` lists library versions for a methods section.
