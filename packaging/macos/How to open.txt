The Great IHC Leap Forward for Mac (Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or newer)

1. Drag "The Great IHC Leap Forward" onto the Applications folder.

2. Open it once from Applications. macOS says it cannot check the app for
   malicious software, because it is not signed with a paid Apple developer
   certificate. Click Done (not Move to Trash).

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about
   "The Great IHC Leap Forward" and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your
   password. From then on it opens normally.

   On macOS 14 (Sonoma) you can instead right-click (or Control-click) the app
   in Applications and choose Open, then Open again.

If macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", open Terminal and run

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/The Great IHC Leap Forward.app"

then open it again.

Studies and settings live in the .ls-pipeline folder in your home folder and
are kept when you replace the app with a newer version.
