# Held back so every library still loads on the macOS the app promises
# (MIN_MACOS in build.sh; check_macos_min.py enforces it).
#
# itk-elastix 0.25.3 and later publish Apple Silicon wheels for macOS 15 only.
itk-elastix<0.25.3

# PySide6 (and shiboken6) wheels are tagged for an older macOS than their
# libraries are built for: 6.10 and 6.11 load only on macOS 15, 6.12 only on
# macOS 26. 6.9 loads on 12 and later. The app needs 6.9 or newer.
PySide6<6.10
PySide6-Essentials<6.10
PySide6-Addons<6.10
shiboken6<6.10
